> service really works. The authors of Netoryx do not operate your service and bear no responsibility for the text you
> publish.

Publish the finished text at a public URL and set it as `TERMS_URL`. To require acceptance before the bot is used, set
`LEGAL_CONSENT_REQUIRED=true`, and change `LEGAL_CONSENT_VERSION` whenever you publish new terms so everyone is asked
again (see [configuration.md](../configuration.md)). Russian version, structured as a public offer:
[ru/legal/terms-of-service-template.md](../ru/legal/terms-of-service-template.md).
